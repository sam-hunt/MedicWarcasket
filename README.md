## About

Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates lets you weld a pawn into a warcasket: a steel shell,
customized part by part at the foundry, with a range of sets that carries a colony from the
industrial era into the late game. This mod adds one more set to that range: the Medic, a
10th-generation spacer-tier warcasket built to keep its pilot patching up squadmates under fire.

The mod is in development: the doctor passives, the mechanite dressing and the adrenaline shot
are in and the set is tuned and priced against VFE Pirates' spacer sets; the descriptions,
sounds and art are still to come.

Odyssey is optional.

## Features

### The Medic Set

Armor, shoulder pads and helmet, welded on at VFE Pirates' warcasket foundry and unlocked by its
spacer warcaskets research.

- **Doctor passives**: the shoulders speed up tending; the helmet raises tend quality and
  surgery success and keeps an impaired pilot's Sight from falling too far
- **Mechanite dressing**: a beam tends every wound on a person or animal, one at a time and
  worst first, using the medicine kits loaded in the armor's suite; the patient is held still
  until it is done. Dressings can be queued, and Shift-click chains patients. With the helmet on,
  it treats diseases and infections too
- **Adrenaline shot**: the shoulders hold a few shots, one medical kit each; one gives a
  person or animal, or the pilot, an adrenaline surge for a few hours, fading in levels while their sleep
  falls faster. A shot into a heart attack stops it instead. With the armor worn, the shot can
  follow every dressing automatically
- **Mod settings** for the medicine, the effect color, the kit capacity, the casting time, the time per
  wound, the cooldown, the range, the shot capacity, what reloads them, their duration, sleep cost
  and cooldown, and which caring work (doctor, childcare) the pilot may take on
- **Priced like its peers**: spacer-tier plating and shield, costs in the tier's band with the
  medicine the suite and injector ship loaded with, and the set's unlock is VFE Pirates' spacer
  warcaskets research
- **Built on VFE Pirates** rather than beside it: the foundry, entombing, customization and
  removal surgery all apply unchanged, and the pieces mix and match with VFE Pirates' own sets

## Requirements

- **RimWorld 1.6** or **1.7**
- **Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates** (required), which itself requires
  **Vanilla Expanded Framework**
- **Harmony** (auto-download from Steam Workshop if you don't have it)
- **Odyssey DLC** is optional

## Installation

### Steam Workshop (Recommended)

Coming with the first release.

### Manual Installation

1. Download the latest release from the [Releases](https://github.com/sam-hunt/MedicWarcasket/releases) page
2. Extract the `MedicWarcasket` folder to your RimWorld `Mods` directory:
   - **Windows**: `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\RimWorld\Mods\`
   - **Mac**: `~/Library/Application Support/Steam/steamapps/common/RimWorld/RimWorldMac.app/Mods/`
   - **Linux**: `~/.steam/steam/steamapps/common/RimWorld/Mods/`
3. Enable the mod in RimWorld's mod menu, after Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates
4. Restart RimWorld

## Compatibility

- **Safe to add** to existing saves.
- **Not safe to remove** from saves while a pawn is welded into the set.
- **Save Our Ship 2 / Universum**: EVA rated under exactly the same rules as VFE Pirates' own sets.
- **Vanilla Gravship Expanded**: follows its vacuum-resistance rules for warcaskets; there is no
  other VGE integration.
- **Combat Extended**: built-in patch. CE armor values, bulk and carry capacity in line with VFE
  Pirates' spacer sets, a helmet sealed against CE smoke, and an adrenaline shot that resists
  suppression as go-juice does. Tending and the medicine kits need no CE patch.
