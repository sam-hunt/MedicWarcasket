Medic Warcasket

A new spacer-tier warcasket for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2723801948]Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates[/url].

[h1]About[/h1]

The Medic is a 10th-generation combat-medic warcasket, built to keep its pilot patching up squadmates under fire.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The Set[/h2]
Armor, shoulder pads and helmet, welded at the warcasket foundry and unlocked by Spacer Warcaskets research. Mix and match the pieces with VFE Pirates' own sets as you please.
[list]
[*][b]Armor:[/b] Grants mechanite dressing, and remains able to do medical work: tending, surgery, feeding and rescue
[*][b]Shoulders:[/b] Grants adrenaline shot, faster tending, and the pilot's manipulation held at full
[*][b]Helmet:[/b] better tend quality, and the pilot's sight held at full
[/list]

[h2]Mechanite dressing[/h2]
The armor carries a suite of medicine kits. Point it at a wounded pawn and the pilot streams mechanites over them in a beam of blue light, tending one wound after another, worst first, at the quality the pilot would give it. The patient is held still until the last wound is done, and the pilot earns medicine experience for each one. With the helmet on, the dressing treats diseases and infections too.

Dressings can be queued, and Shift-click chains several patients in a row. Drag the suite's gauge to set how full colonists keep it. The medicine it uses, the number of kits, the timings and which work the pilot may do can be changed in the mod settings.

[h2]Adrenaline shot[/h2]
The shoulders hold a few shots of adrenaline, drawn from medical kits. Jab a squadmate, a pet or the pilot and they feel almost no pain and move, see and think faster for a few hours, the surge fading in levels while their sleep runs down faster than usual. No addiction, no tolerance. A shot into a heart attack is spent on the heart instead and stops it outright. With the armor worn, right-click the shot to give one to every patient as their dressing ends. The shot capacity, what reloads them, how long one lasts, its sleep cost and its cooldown are mod settings.
