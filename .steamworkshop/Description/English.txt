Medic Warcasket

A new spacer-tier warcasket for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2723801948]Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates[/url].

[h1]About[/h1]

The Medic is a 10th-generation combat-medic warcasket, built to keep its pilot patching up squadmates under fire.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The Set[/h2]
Armor, shoulders and helmet, welded at the warcasket foundry and unlocked by spacer warcaskets research. Mix and match the pieces with VFE Pirates' own sets as you please.
[list]
[*][b]Armor:[/b] Grants mechanite dressing and leaves the pilot free to doctor: tending, surgery, feeding and rescue
[*][b]Shoulders:[/b] Grants the adrenaline shot and faster tending, and keeps the pilot's manipulation at full
[*][b]Helmet:[/b] Grants better tend quality, and keeps the pilot's sight at full
[/list]

[h2]Mechanite dressing[/h2]
The armor carries a suite of medicine kits. Point it at a wounded pawn and the pilot engages the suit's mechanite-powered medical array, quickly tending each wound, worst first, at the pilot's medical quality. With the helmet's diagnostic visor, disease and infection are treatable too.

[h2]Adrenaline shot[/h2]
The shoulders hold a few shots of adrenaline that can cure heart attacks or provide a last-resort measure for downed pawns to escape danger. Pain is put on hold, and the pawn gains a large movement boost for a short time. The surge fades progressively into a long crash, similar to real life. With the armor worn together, right-click/autocast administers it automatically to every patient when mechanite dressing completes.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]

[b]Q: How do I get it?[/b]
A: Research spacer warcaskets, then weld it on at the warcasket foundry like any other set.

[b]Q: Is this imbalanced?[/b]
A: Abilities are fully tunable in mod settings; I put effort into picking reasonable defaults though.

[b]Q: What inspired this?[/b]
A: An arguable gap in the roster, too many dead colonists, and thousands of hours of childhood Starcraft.
