Medic Warcasket

A new spacer-tier warcasket for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2723801948]Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates[/url].

[h1]About[/h1]

The Medic is a 10th-generation combat medic warcasket, built to keep its pilot patching up squadmates under fire.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The Set[/h2]
Armor, shoulders and helmet, built at the warcasket foundry and unlocked by spacer warcaskets research. Mix and match the pieces with VFE Pirates' own sets as you please.
[list]
[*][b]Armor:[/b] Grants mechanite dressing, and the pilot can still do doctor work: tending, surgery, feeding and rescue.
[*][b]Shoulders:[/b] Grants the adrenaline shot and faster tending, and keeps the pilot's manipulation at full.
[*][b]Helmet:[/b] Grants better tend quality, and keeps the pilot's sight at full.
[/list]

[h2]Mechanite dressing[/h2]
The armor carries a suite of medicine kits. Point it at a wounded pawn and the pilot streams mechanites over them, tending each wound starting with the worst, at the pilot's own tend quality. With the helmet's diagnostic visor, it treats disease and infection too.

[h2]Adrenaline shot[/h2]
The shoulders hold a few shots of adrenaline. A shot stops a heart attack, or gets a downed pawn back on their feet long enough to escape. Pain is put on hold, and the pawn moves and thinks faster for a short time. The surge fades progressively into a long crash, similar to real life. With the armor worn, autocast gives every patient a shot as their dressing ends.

[h2]Stranger in black[/h2]
Optionally in mod settings, allow the stranger to wear one, so that late game defeats don't necessarily mean the end.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]

[b]Q: How do I get it?[/b]
A: Research spacer warcaskets, then build it at the warcasket foundry like any other set.

[b]Q: Is this imbalanced?[/b]
A: Abilities are fully tunable in mod settings; I put effort into picking reasonable defaults though.

[b]Q: What inspired this?[/b]
A: An opportunistic gap in the warcasket roster, too many dead colonists, and perhaps thousands of hours of childhood Starcraft.
